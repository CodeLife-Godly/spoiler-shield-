# Privacy Policy — Spoiler Shield

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

Spoiler Shield hides webpage content that matches the GTA VI terms enabled by default or the custom keywords you choose.

## Information processed
The extension examines text in webpages you visit so it can identify matching terms and hide matching page sections. This text processing happens locally in your browser. The extension does not send webpage text, browsing history, custom keywords, or personal information to a developer-operated server.

## Settings
The extension stores your on/off setting, GTA VI filter setting, blur-or-remove preference, list of skipped sites and custom keyword list using Chrome's `storage.sync` facility. If Chrome Sync is enabled for your account, Chrome may synchronize these settings according to your Chrome settings and Google's policies.

## Current tab
When you open the popup, the extension reads the address of the active tab to show the site name and let you skip that site. This stays in your browser and is not stored except for any sites you choose to skip.

## Data sharing and analytics
The extension does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party network requests. The developer does not collect or sell personal data through the extension.

## Retention and deletion
Your settings remain in Chrome storage until you change them, remove keywords, or uninstall the extension. You can remove custom keywords in the popup. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored extension data subject to Chrome's own sync behavior.

## Children's privacy
The extension is a general-purpose content filter and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If the extension's behavior or data practices change, this policy should be updated. Before publishing, add a real contact email and host this policy at a public URL. Do not publish this draft unchanged if any statement no longer accurately describes the submitted build.
