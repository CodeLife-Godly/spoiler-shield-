# Spoiler Shield — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

## Overview

Spoiler Shield is a browser extension that helps users avoid GTA VI spoilers and other unwanted keywords while browsing the web.

## Information Collection

Spoiler Shield does not collect, sell, or transmit users' personal information to the developer or to third parties.

## Website Content

The extension locally examines webpage text to identify content matching GTA VI spoiler terms and keywords chosen by the user. This processing takes place within the user's browser. Website text is not sent to external servers by the extension.

## Locally Stored Settings

The extension uses the browser's storage API to save user preferences, such as the spoiler-blocking toggle and custom keywords. These settings are stored within the browser and are not transmitted to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

Spoiler Shield does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or external data-collection services.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or privacy practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this URL.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the extension developer through the support contact listed on the Spoiler Shield store listing.
